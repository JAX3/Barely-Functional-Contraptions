These are the ideas from the earlier roadmap that haven’t been built yet. Steps 1, 7, 15, 16 and 17 are done, and part of step 5 (the Glow Panel and Glow Lamp).

Logistics
2. Pipe tiers: copper, then iron, then netherite pipes that move more per tick, made with upgrade items or by crafting.
3. Wool Barrel or drawer: a block that stores large amounts of one item, for resource wool, which builds up fast.
4. Sheep transport: a Sheep Crate or catching net that picks up an infused sheep and keeps its infusion, skin and stats until you place it again.
Power
5. The rest of the themed generators:
Wool Burner: burns plain or spare wool for power.
Blaze Sheep Furnace: makes power from the Blaze sheep’s flames.
6. Energy tiers or a capacitor item that charges tools, plus a wireless charger.
Resource generation
8. Sheep stats from breeding: yield, growth speed and how long an infusion lasts go up as you breed good sheep together, like the Chickens mod.
9. Resource mutations: breeding two resources makes a new one (for example Iron + Gold gives Copper), so rare resources become things to work towards.
10. Tiers of infusion: the Wither, Dragon and Sculk sheep need a better feeding station or a ritual, not just one item.
11. Sheep Feeder block: infuses and re-feeds sheep in range on its own, so a pen runs without you.
12. Auto-breeder and lamb sorter: like Industrial Foregoing’s rancher and baby separator.
13. Separator upgrades: speed, energy use and a chance of extra output, plus a better processing step (for example wool into fibre, giving 4–6 resource instead of 2–4).
14. Fluid sheep: themed sheep that give buckets of lava, water or honey, which can go into the Fluid Tank.
